## Method and limitations

The labels and descriptions in this report were supplied by the user. No independent checks were performed against current map listings, official business pages, physical signage, business registries, opening hours, or dated imagery. Descriptions such as “popular,” “prominent,” “luxury,” “24 hours,” “permanently closed,” and “temporarily closed” remain source claims rather than verified findings.

Where multiple businesses are listed together in one building or commercial space, their co-location and relationships have not been independently established. A combined label is not automatically a single entity. Business names and map labels are metadata candidates, not ground truth. No private-person identity or property ownership is inferred. No Street View imagery is redistributed.
