## Cross-batch methodological results

### 1. Longitudinal slot remains n=0

### 2. Shared functional category is not a pairing key

B51 rejects the inference that two education-related institutions with apparent pole/tree anchors share one error mechanism. B52 rejects the reverse inference that two institutions apparently anchored on their own structures establish a “correctly anchored institutions” rule. The same principle applies in both directions: shared category does not establish shared entity, shared coordinate, or shared placement mechanism.

### 3. Appearance at a pole, tree, canopy, roof, or neighboring frontage is two-directional

A pin that appears to overlap a pole/tree/canopy may be a projection effect or a legitimate access/address anchor. A pin near a branded neighboring tenant may reflect a neighboring-unit/co-tenancy arrangement or a placement problem. The supplied single-view descriptions cannot resolve these alternatives.

### 4. Reappearance is not automatically longitudinal evidence

A same-POI reappearance with identical metadata does not demonstrate metadata drift. A different-looking scene does not demonstrate an independent capture pass when date labels are the same and session separation is unknown. For a future drift comparison, a prior record must demonstrably change or disappear in a comparable later observation. For a projection test, separate capture passes and coordinate correspondence must be established rather than inferred from different-looking scenes alone.

### 5. Scene text and UI artifacts are not evidence of pin accuracy

The ZOSS sign, Alfamart promotions, flags, generic shop signs, and road signs do not provide an independent date cross-check. Graduation-cap, lock, fork, and other icons are retained as separate UI/ghost context where reported; they are not reassigned to the selected POI without evidence.

### 6. Listing metadata is not spatial ground truth

Ratings, review counts (including 565), categories, opening hours, “Temporarily closed” status, and route-duration values are retained as displayed listing metadata. They do not validate coordinates, establish current operation, or prove a mismatch.
