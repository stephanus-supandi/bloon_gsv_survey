## New service claim: international sea and air freight forwarding

A banner on a colorful shop-house is reported to read:

> “The International Sea & Air Freight Forwarder”

This is a new **sign-level claim** of international freight-forwarding services by sea and air. It is distinct from previously recorded local delivery/supply activity and from migrant-worker placement services.

The evidence is the wording on a banner. It does not establish that the business is operating, licensed, connected to an international network, or actually handling shipments. It must not be joined to the migrant-worker agency observation as a single “export” story, nor generalized into a claim that the neighborhood is a global trade hub.

## New public-legitimacy vector: age claim on a directional sign

A green sign on a weathered wooden fence is reported to read:

> “ASJID JAMI AL HUDA / DIDIRIKAN TAHUN 1911”

The sign includes a left-pointing arrow and an approximate distance of 50 metres.

The new observation is **institutional age presented as a public claim**, communicated through a directional sign before the visitor reaches the institution. This differs from previously recorded place-of-origin claims, administrative identifiers, and legal-entity plaques.

The year 1911 is recorded as a claim on the sign, not as independently verified founding history. The old-looking fence, typography, or spelling does not authenticate the date. Al Huda is not merged with Pondok Pesantren Mahasina; they are separate institutions.

## Cross-date caution

The Mahasina listing is reported as 4.9/262 in this May 2024 snapshot and was also reported with the same displayed rating/count in a February 2025 batch. Identical values in two snapshots do not establish that the count remained stable throughout the interval. The two observations remain separate; no continuity claim is made.
