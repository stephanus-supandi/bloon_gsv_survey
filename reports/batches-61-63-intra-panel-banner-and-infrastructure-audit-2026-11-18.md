## Batch 61 — Intra-panel name mismatch and an orphanage institution

### Wrinkle 1: title/subtitle variation inside one panel

The f1 panel is reported to show:

- Title: **Klinik Mitra Al Afiat**
- Subtitle: **Klinik Mitra 2 Al-Afiat**

The difference occurs within one reported panel/render, rather than across separate poses or tiles. It extends the name-variation family to **intra-panel field variation**.

This does not by itself establish multiple records, a database merge, or a structural database failure. The two strings may be alternate display fields for one entity. The cause and intended canonical name remain unknown.

**Classification:** intra-panel name-field variation; cause unresolved. Distinct from the multi-record/same-facade observation in Batch 54.

### Wrinkle 2: religious/social-service context extends beyond retail

f5 is reported to show **Panti Asuhan Daarul Aitam**, with category **Orphanage**.

This extends the previously observed religiously inflected retail/economic context (carpets, prayer goods, dates, religious-book printing, and related shops) into a social-care institution. The name is a self-reported text token and is homonym-prone. It does not establish a unique institution, ownership, religious affiliation, or camera position.

An orphanage is not automatically a state institution. It should not be grouped with state identifiers such as public projects or military facilities merely because it is institutional.

## Batch 63 — Multiple POI representations within one reported SPBU facility

### Wrinkle 1: functional sub-entity represented alongside the main facility

The observer reports two pins within the same visible Pertamina filling-station facility:

- f3: **Musholla SPBU Pertamina 34 10203**, associated with a red Pertamina totem.
- f4: **PERTAnami**, associated with a red Pertamina canopy.

This is recorded as **multi-entity POI representation within a shared physical facility**. A filling station and a prayer room can legitimately be distinct points of interest within one complex. Their coexistence does not by itself prove duplicate records, faulty database fragmentation, or an incorrect entity count.

The reported “PERTAnami” string is retained as an unresolved name/rendering token. Its segmentation, intended spelling, or meaning is not inferred.

The musholla adds a religious-use facility within an energy-infrastructure context. It does not establish that Pertamina policy requires such facilities; only the reported listing and visual association are recorded.

### Wrinkle 2: several unlike panel fields in the PERTAnami listing

The f4 panel is reported to contain:

- Status: **Temporarily closed**
- Rating: **1.0 (5)**

The review wording appears semantically incongruent with the reported fuel-station context. Possible explanations—including historical food service, stale or mismatched review context, nearby-business association, or display behavior—remain untested.

This is classified as **cross-field semantic incongruence within one POI panel**, not proof of hallucination, database corruption, or an incorrectly attached review. The low rating is an aggregate platform value and provides no geographic evidence. “Temporarily closed” is a listing status, not independent proof of physical closure at the time of imagery capture.

## Cross-batch method notes

1. **Camera ground truth remains zero for Batches 61–63.** No reported item independently verifies the camera's exact position.
2. **Different fields within one panel are not automatically separate records.** The Klinik Mitra title/subtitle variation is an intra-panel field difference, not the same mechanism as the multi-record/same-facade case discussed for Batch 54.
3. **A single spelling anomaly is not a database-merge event.** “Seefood” is recorded as one reported orthographic anomaly; its source and cause are unknown.
4. **Uncertain physical boundaries remain uncertain.** The INDAH/ANDA EXPRES fabric observation does not establish whether one or two banners are present.
5. **A shared facility can contain distinct POIs.** A prayer room and a filling station may be separately listed without implying erroneous fragmentation.
6. **Semantic mismatch is not proof of hallucination.** Review prose that appears unrelated to a POI category should be retained as unresolved cross-field incongruence until its provenance is known.
7. **Printed names, road paint, banners, and institutional labels are self-reported physical text.** They can support a narrative context but do not independently locate the camera.
8. **Travel-time values are relational metadata.** Values such as 52–56 minutes do not reveal bearing, straight-line distance, coordinates, or panorama pose.
9. **Ratings, review counts, categories, opening hours, and status fields are platform metadata.** Their unusual values should be preserved without assuming the underlying cause.
10. **Chrome clock, battery, and companion glyphs are viewer/device evidence.** They are not camera telemetry.
11. **Jakarta remains a strong narrative hypothesis, not verified camera ground truth.** Mas Mansyur, Kebon Kacang, Tanah Abang, Thamrin City, Pertamina, and related tokens are not converted into an exact camera location by accumulation alone.
12. **Institutional terminology correction:** KOWAL stands for **Korps Wanita Angkatan Laut** (Indonesian Navy Women's Corps), not the Army. This corrects the branch attribution in the earlier discussion of the Kesatrian Kowal Cut Nyak Dien sign.
