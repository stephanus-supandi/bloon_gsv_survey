## Escalations, tests, and rejected pairings

### E11 — Shared name token across continents and functional categories

The token “Hitler” is displayed in four frames across two continents: Ohio road/geocode name strings (Img1, Img2, Img4) and an Indian clothing-store listing (Img5). This is **not** evidence of one global entity, a global map pattern, cross-continent co-location, or a longitudinal track.

The functional categories differ: “Hitler Rd” is a road/address string, while “The Hitler garments” is a clothing-store listing. A shared token does not establish shared entity identity. The continents and reported imagery eras also differ (Ohio, Aug 2024; India, Apr 2026), and no coordinate, record identifier, or independent evidence connects them. Possible origins of the shared word are not investigated here and remain unknown. The permitted observation is only that the same text token appears in multiple unrelated-looking fields. **E11 ceiling: zero**—not a longitudinal test and not evidence of a global pattern.

### E12 — “Hitler Rd 2” string recurrence with different rendering states

Img1 displays a red pin against sky; Img4 does not show a red pin but does display “Hitler Rd 2” as road-surface label text. The session boundary and record identity are unresolved. The difference is consistent with multiple benign readings: different camera perspectives/rendering states, separate passes in the same area, or a misread frame. Absence of a rendered pin does not establish absence of a POI, and the road label does not substitute for a pin. This is not evidence that a pin moved, not proof of two coordinates, and not a verified projection test. **E12 ceiling: zero**—string-level recurrence only.
