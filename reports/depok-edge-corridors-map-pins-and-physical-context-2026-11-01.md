## 1. Scope and evidence discipline

This report consolidates the supplied Depok batches. It records visible objects, displayed map labels, and the apparent spatial relationship between pins and physical objects.

The following distinctions are mandatory:

- A map label or pin is not proof that a business physically occupies the point where the pin appears.
- A pin's apparent overlap with a roof, tree, sign, cart, fence, or empty lot is a visual relationship in the displayed image, not proof of physical attachment.
- A displayed rating, review count, category, or opening-hours status is platform metadata, not independent confirmation of service quality, activity, or continuous operation.
- Absence of a visible street-name sign in the supplied frames does not establish whether a sign was never installed, removed, obscured, or located outside the frame.
- Explanations for mismatches between map pins and physical locations remain unknown unless independently verified.

No inference is made about ownership, business relationships, religious character, political affiliation, or the reason a location is pinned.

These are descriptive findings from the supplied observations, not statistical claims about Depok as a whole.
