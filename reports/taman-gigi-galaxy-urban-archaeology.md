## 1. Purpose

This note records the documentary trail for Taman Gigi in Taman Aster, Kelurahan Jakasetia, Bekasi Selatan. It is an evidence ledger, not a cadastral survey, legal history, or claim that every current facility and map label has been independently verified.

The working principle is:

> GOBLOK BOLEH. NGARANG JANGAN.

Keep contemporaneous reporting, government datasets, personal recollection, map observations, and hypotheses in separate evidence classes.

## 2. Findings supported by sources

### 2.1 Planning and original purpose — 25 January 2016

ANTARA reported that the Bekasi City Government, through the Health Office, was planning a Taman Gigi in Taman Aster, RW 014, Kelurahan Jakasetia, Kecamatan Bekasi Selatan. The report described a partnership with Persatuan Dokter Gigi Indonesia (PDGI), an educational purpose focused especially on children, a tooth-shaped icon, dental-health information, and play facilities.

The article described 2 March 2016 as the *planned* launch date. A planned date must not be treated as proof that the event occurred on that date.

Source: [ANTARA — Pemkot Bekasi Rancang Pembuatan Taman Edukasi Gigi, 25 January 2016](https://megapolitan.antaranews.com/berita/19108/pemkot-bekasi-rancang-pembuatan-taman-edukasi-gigi)

**Assessment:** Planning, location at Taman Aster RW 014, educational purpose, and reported PDGI partnership are supported by contemporaneous reporting.

### 2.2 Existence and inauguration inscription — reported in January 2017

Ayo Bandung reported that its journalist visited the park on 18 January 2017. The article described trees, grass, benches, a tooth statue, and information boards about oral anatomy, brushing, and dental development.

The report also said an inscription at the park stated that it was presented by PDGI Kota Bekasi and inaugurated by Mayor Rahmat Effendi on 6 March 2016.

Source: [Ayo Bandung — PDGI Kota Bekasi Buat Taman Bertema Kesehatan Mulut dan Gigi, 19 January 2017](https://www.ayobandung.com/nasional/pr-79617188/topik-khusus.html)

**Assessment:** The park's existence and educational features by January 2017 are supported by a contemporaneous visit report. The 6 March 2016 inauguration date is **corroborated by the article's report of the inscription**, but no signed inauguration decree or original event record has been located in this research pass. Do not describe it as legally certified.

### 2.3 A contemporaneous institutional-name discrepancy — November 2016

A separate ANTARA report on Bekasi's thematic-park program referred to Taman Gigi in Perumahan Galaxy and attributed the collaboration to **Ikatan Dokter Indonesia (IDI)**, rather than PDGI.

Source: [ANTARA — 1.000 Taman Bekasi Ingin Miliki Tema, 11 November 2016](https://megapolitan.antaranews.com/berita/25405/1000-taman-bekasi-ingin-miliki-tema)

This differs from the January 2016 ANTARA report and the January 2017 Ayo Bandung report, both of which name PDGI. Preserve the discrepancy. The two PDGI references provide stronger repeated support for PDGI involvement, but the IDI wording in the November 2016 report remains part of the source record and should not be silently edited away.

### 2.4 Administrative location — 2022 municipal sectoral data

The Kota Bekasi *Profil Data Sektoral Kota Bekasi Tahun 2022* lists two entries named **TAMAN GIGI**, both with the address:

**Jl. Taman Aster Blok L No.4 Jaka Setia, Kecamatan Bekasi Selatan, Kelurahan Jakasetia.**

Source: [Profil Data Sektoral Kota Bekasi Tahun 2022 — PDF](https://portalsatudata.bekasikota.go.id/upload/file/6850abe6175927268086a35431f41e84.pdf)

**Assessment:** This is a government sectoral-data reference for the park's listed administrative address. The duplicate rows should be preserved as they appear in the source; they do not prove there are two separate parks.

The address supplied in the initial investigation — Jl. Taman Aster No.4, RT.010/RW.014 — is shorter and does not include “Blok L”. The government table supports “Blok L No.4” and Kelurahan Jakasetia, but the table does not establish RT/RW. Do not assume the two address forms are identical in every administrative detail until checked against an authoritative address record.

## 4. Spatial interpretation rules

The working BLOON GSV Survey diagram uses schematic cells, including references to:

- Taman Gigi Galaksi — A3/A4
- Coboy Jeans Custom — A4
- DENTIS Dental Care — A4
- Taman Aster / Sakura corridor
- Jl. Nakula Raya — A5

These are **working-map labels**, not independently verified coordinates, parcel boundaries, or proof of adjacency. A shared grid cell does not establish that two points are next door, that one business replaced another, or that the places share a historical relationship.

Before making a spatial claim, retain and compare:
1. Original map or Street View permalink;
2. Capture date / imagery date, kept distinct;
3. Visible fixed landmarks and camera direction;
4. Address or parcel evidence where available;
5. Explicit match status: confirmed, candidate, or unresolved.

## 5. Relationship to the wider urban-archaeology project

Taman Gigi is a useful **public-space and community-history anchor** for a wider study of Taman Galaxy and Jakasetia. It provides a documentary entry point into Bekasi's thematic-park program and public-space planning around 2016.

It does **not**, by itself, establish:
- the founding date of the Galaxy residential area;
- a continuous history from Mas Naga to present-day businesses;
- the opening date of every commercial or residential development;
- the historical relationship between businesses that appear close on a schematic map;
- a complete chronology of Taman Aster, Sakura, or Nakula Raya.

The wider project should be built as a set of separately sourced case records, not one uninterrupted narrative assembled from map proximity.

## 6. Cross-checking and audit method

Independent AI reviews (Gemini and Qwen) were treated as **research aids, not evidence sources**. Their outputs were checked against dated media reports and the municipal sectoral dataset.

This audit found that a claim of “no contemporaneous 2016 documentation” would be too strong: ANTARA published a planning report on 25 January 2016. The later Ayo Bandung visit report also provides a contemporaneous description and reports the inauguration inscription.

AI-generated summaries, search snippets, map labels, and directory listings must not replace the underlying source. When an AI review misses a source, correct the audit trail rather than treating the absence in that review as proof that no source exists.

## 8. Current conclusion

Taman Gigi Galaxy is a documented thematic public-space project in Taman Aster, Jakasetia. Its planning and intended educational purpose were reported by ANTARA in January 2016; a January 2017 report documented the park's visible features and reported an inauguration inscription dated 6 March 2016; and Kota Bekasi's 2022 sectoral dataset lists the park at Jl. Taman Aster Blok L No.4, Jaka Setia, Kelurahan Jakasetia.

That is enough to establish Taman Gigi as a **documentary anchor for BLOON Urban Archaeology**. It is not enough to turn every nearby map label, business listing, personal recollection, or neighborhood-origin story into verified history.
