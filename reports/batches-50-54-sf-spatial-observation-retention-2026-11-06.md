## Method notes carried forward

1. **Visual proximity is not causation.** A skyline, fence, cable, storefront, and business can appear together without proving an economic or political relationship.
2. **A single frame is not a neighborhood census.** It cannot establish who lives, works, eats, sleeps, or is excluded from the visible area.
3. **Platform metadata is not ground truth.** Ratings, review counts, price bands, hours, labels, and AI summaries require their own provenance and interpretation.
4. **A covered storefront is a visible condition, not a known business status.** Record the material state; leave the underlying function unresolved until independently checked.
5. **Keep capture dates separate.** Do not narratively stitch frames from December 2024, February 2025, and September 2026 into a single simultaneous scene.
6. **Duplicate batches are valid audit outcomes.** “No new finding” is a result, not a failure.
7. **The observer is also a framing agent.** BLOON reports are representations assembled through selection, categorization, and language. Their claims must be audited against the source observations.
