### 4.5 General pairing rule

The retained rule is generative, not a list of forbidden words or numbers:

> Do not pair records as one tracked entity unless there is evidence of the same coordinate/physical place, or a defensible cross-date record history in which the same entity is tracked and the earlier record is absent at the later date. Similar strings, generic phrases, third-party banners, matching route durations, ratings, or visual proximity do not substitute for that evidence.

The B47 Rohmah correction remains in force: the apparent Bedding store / Repair service difference is not a verified one-record metadata drift because the two records were reported co-existing in the same January 2026 session. The prior longitudinal drift claim remains withdrawn.
