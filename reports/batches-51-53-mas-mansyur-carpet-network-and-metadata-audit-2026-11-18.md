## Consolidated method notes

1. **Camera ground truth remains zero.** No reported element in Batches 51–53 independently verifies the camera's exact position.
2. **Road-plus-alley network is a content structure, not a lens fix.** Carpet signage in an alley context extends the observed narrative but does not map a verified business or property network.
3. **Travel time is relational metadata, not radius.** Values such as 52–53 minutes do not reveal bearing, straight-line distance, coordinates, or panorama pose.
4. **Listing fields require field-specific caution.** Categories, opening hours, review summaries, ratings, and POI names are platform-rendered data. Unusual values should be retained as anomalies or unresolved fields, not automatically called errors or lies.
5. **Repeated observations are not automatically independent verification.** Similar values across batches may share a data source, UI mechanism, origin, or other dependency that has not been established.
6. **Chrome clock, battery, and companion icons are viewer/device UI evidence.** They are not camera telemetry.
7. **Geographic accumulation remains narrative, not ground.** The reported Mas Mansyur, Kebon Kacang, Al Habsyi, Said Na'um, MONAS, sewerage-project, institutional, and business-name clues make a Jakarta interpretation increasingly coherent. They do not, by accumulation alone, verify the lens location.
8. **Plateau is provisional.** Most observations in these batches extend existing categories. This is a current description of the audit sequence, not a rule that future batches cannot contain a genuinely new mechanism or contradiction.
