## Cross-batch evidence limits

The following rules remain in force:

1. **Platform labels are metadata.** POI names, categories, ratings, review counts, opening-hours/status strings, subtitles, and travel-time/radius fields do not independently establish physical location.
2. **Printed or painted text is self-report.** A road name, business banner, institutional sign, founding-year claim, or house number may be recorded as visible text, but does not by itself prove camera position or entity identity.
3. **Visual correspondence is not geospatial anchoring.** Repeated appearance of a billboard or other object can support candidate object matching across tiles, but not coordinates, pose order, or a continuous camera path.
4. **Panel variation is not database corruption.** Rich/poor/empty panel differences are representation observations unless independently verified against the underlying data.
5. **Status-bar chrome is not capture provenance.** Clock and battery readings are not Street View capture timestamps and cannot independently establish session boundaries.
6. **Ambiguity is retained.** No uncertain business-to-banner association, city assignment, subtitle transformation, or background activity is forced into a definitive interpretation.
