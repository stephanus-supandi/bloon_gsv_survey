# Dallas — SMU Boulevard & George W. Bush Presidential Center
**Field note date:** 2026-11-02  
**Project:** BLOON GSV Archaeology / visual Street View survey  
**Status:** Preliminary visual observations and classification correction  
**Evidence basis:** User-described five-frame batch. Original screenshots/raw image files were not attached to this report.

## 1. Main correction: lettering painted on the road

The earlier working distinction between **“names on the road”** and **“directions on the road”** was too narrow. The Dallas frame showing **“SMU Boulevard”** painted prominently in the traffic lane prompted a correction.

Across the compared batches, the same physical medium — lettering painted on the road surface — has at least three observed communicative uses:

1. **Place or street naming** — e.g. “Donald Trump Ave” in the Hyderabad comparison.
2. **Traffic instruction** — e.g. “THRU DRIVE” / “ONLY UP” in the Florida comparison.
3. **Institution or campus identification** — “SMU Boulevard” in Dallas.

This is a classification of the described examples, not a claim that the three functions are exhaustive or mutually exclusive. The earlier Florida distinction is revised: road-surface lettering should be classified by its communicative function, not split into separate media merely because one example names a place and another gives an instruction.

The revised approach treats both as road-surface lettering and classifies each instance by function. Dallas adds the campus/institution-identification example (“SMU Boulevard”) to the comparison.
