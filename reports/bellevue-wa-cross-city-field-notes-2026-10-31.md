## 1. Purpose

This note extends the BLOON GSV survey beyond Indonesia to a cross-city comparison involving Bellevue, Washington, USA, and locations in Bekasi / greater Jakarta, Indonesia.

The purpose is not to rank cities or infer the character of their residents. It is to record visible urban features, possible changes in land use, and map-listing anomalies while keeping observations separate from interpretations.

## 2. Evidence and limitations

The descriptions below were supplied by the user during the conversation. The underlying screenshots are not embedded in this Markdown record. Accordingly:

- Visual details are recorded as **reported observations**, not as independently inspected evidence.
- Google Maps / Street View labels, business categories, ratings, and closure statuses are platform metadata. They do not by themselves establish ownership, legal status, historical use, or the identity of an occupant.
- A repeated screenshot is one repeated image, not a new independent observation.
- Similar names do not establish organizational or commercial relationships.
- A visual impression of vacancy does not establish the date, cause, or permanence of closure.
- No building height, ground elevation, road gradient, or survey-grade measurement is inferred from perspective alone.

## 6. Suggested verification workflow

For each site or claim:

1. Preserve the original screenshot and record its source URL or Street View panorama reference where permitted.
2. Record the displayed imagery date separately from the screenshot capture date.
3. Record the exact visible street name, address, and map pin position, without inferring an occupant's identity.
4. Compare imagery from multiple dates at the same viewpoint.
5. Check official planning, property, business, or municipal records when making claims about land use, ownership, closure, or redevelopment.
6. Mark each item as one of:
   - `REPORTED` — supplied by the user or another observer.
   - `OBSERVED` — directly visible in an available source image.
   - `CORROBORATED` — supported by independent reliable evidence.
   - `DISPUTED` — conflicting evidence exists.
   - `UNKNOWN` — not currently established.
   - `NOT_VERIFIED` — verification has not been performed.

## 7. Measurement caution: imagery is not a survey

Street View perspective, aerial imagery, and 3D visualization can help inspect urban form, but they are not interchangeable with surveyed measurements.

Do not infer:
- Ground elevation from a perspective screenshot.
- Building height from apparent pixel size alone.
- Road slope from a single oblique view.
- Drone capture merely because an image is elevated.
- Precise distances without a known scale or geospatial measurement method.

For elevation or engineering-grade measurements, identify the underlying DEM, LiDAR, photogrammetric product, survey datum, resolution, and accuracy. Record the source and method.
