## Evidence status and scope

This is a structured archive of user-supplied descriptions of Google Maps / Street View screenshots and prior visual notes. It is **not an independent re-inspection of the screenshots, a live Google Maps check, or proof of business status or location identity**. Every entry below remains `UNVERIFIED_FROM_SOURCE_NOTES` until the underlying screenshot, capture date, camera position, map pin, and visible physical anchor are reviewed together.

The labels “ghost pin,” “identity swap,” “category drift,” “floating pin,” “coordinate collision,” and similar phrases are **working hypotheses / informal anomaly tags**, not established causes or proof that Google’s systems are defective. A pin rendered over a pole, road, vehicle, wall, or neighboring building may reflect camera perspective, label rendering, geocoding, stale data, a home-based business, an entrance offset, or another explanation. A screenshot alone generally cannot establish which explanation is correct.

Ratings and review counts do not establish whether reviews are genuine. Do not infer self-reviews, fraud, ownership, business relationships, or intent from a high/low rating or small review count. “Permanently closed” and “temporarily closed” are recorded only as statuses reported in the supplied notes, not independently checked current statuses.
