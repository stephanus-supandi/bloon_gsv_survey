Script recognition or a plausible language association is not a coordinate, camera pose, or verified place identity. India/Telugu-related material is also not a new continental discovery in this corpus; India-related tiles were already reported in Batches 24–29.

**Classification:** render-medium/canvas extension; assembly mechanism unresolved; no geospatial verification.
