## Escalations and interpretation limits

### E18 — Combined benign-pattern reinforcement

B71 is reported as the first occurrence in the reviewed register where two previously separate benign patterns appear together:

- School and mosque appear adjacent within one apparent compound (Img1–Img2).
- Both named POIs reportedly reappear across batches: SMPN 252 from B51 and Masjid AT-Taqwa from B52.

The combination may create an impression of one confirmed compound tracked across time. That conclusion is not supported. Co-location/adjacency in one reported scene is not proof of a single entity; reappearance without evidence of the same coordinate across distinct capture passes is not a longitudinal test. The two observations concern different evidentiary questions and neither independently establishes coordinate truth. Their co-occurrence does not convert them into coordinate evidence: **co-occurrence of two benign patterns is not a conjunction of proof**.

Even if the school and mosque are in fact part of one compound, that would not itself establish one POI tracked longitudinally. The supplied material does not establish one entity observed across independent passes, nor a record disappearing across dates. The reported anchor appearances are described as consistent with the earlier examples, but the earlier comparison frames are not available here for pixel-level review. Therefore no measurable drift signal is established; absence of a measurable change is not evidence of stability.

**E18 classification:** methodological escalation only; no coordinate evidence, no longitudinal test, no tier increase.

The two reported reappearances are precisely the POIs previously cited as illustrative examples in B51 and B52. This creates a distinct familiarity/baseline illusion: a contributor may remember an entity from the register and mistake that familiarity for prior verification.

The earlier entries were illustrative observations of rendered pin appearance, not field-verified coordinates. A citation as an example is not coordinate verification. The current intake also lacks the B51/B52 frames for direct pixel-position comparison and does not establish distinct capture passes. Reappearance is not equivalent to a record disappearing across dates, and it does not establish the same coordinate on separate passes.

**E19 classification:** methodological escalation only; not verified continuity, not a drift baseline, and not a longitudinal test.
