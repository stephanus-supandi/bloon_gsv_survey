## Cross-batch conclusions and limits

1. **Batch composition is not a reliable spatial unit.** Subset/reorder (113) and hybrid corridor composition (115) show that a batch cannot automatically be treated as one coherent capture package or route.
2. **Do not over-specify the mechanism.** “Batch-assembly artifact” describes the observed composition. Cache, manual selection, backend assembly, or other causes remain hypotheses without system logs.
3. **Clock is not epoch or session proof.** Batch 114 and 115 reinforce that displayed clock/battery values cannot establish imagery chronology or session identity.
4. **Same name is not same entity.** Batch 114’s Agen Pos Shada Inc recurrence remains unresolved physical identity.
5. **No geospatial upgrade.** Lens-verified camera position: **zero**.
