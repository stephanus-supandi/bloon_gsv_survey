## Temporal comparison rules

1. Record the imagery date displayed by Street View; do not confuse it with the date the screenshot was captured.
2. Compare fixed visual anchors before claiming that two images show the same property.
3. Treat a building visible in two dates as evidence of persistence only if identity is established.
4. Do not infer a business opened after 2018 merely because its sign is absent from one older frame; the view may be incomplete or obscured.
5. Separate map labels and listing metadata from visible physical evidence.
6. Record duplicate frames once, but preserve alternate viewpoints when they add spatial context.
