## Repeat and ambiguity controls

1. **Toko Sepeda SETIA** appears in multiple summaries, with both bicycle retail and repair/service descriptions. Keep one provisional entity until signage, frontage, and position are compared.
2. **Romantis Tailor** and **RM Ampera Amak Jatiwaringin** also recur. Repetition in summaries does not establish multiple locations.
3. **Sate Cahaya Jingga**, **Alfamidi Pondok Uringin**, and other names recur across earlier Jatiwaringin summaries. Treat as repeat mentions pending exact image/pin comparison.
4. **Telor Martabak Borneo / Telur Martabak Borneo** has a spelling variation. Preserve both variants in provenance; do not silently normalize without checking the sign.
5. Generic names such as **Wibowo**, **Toko Wati**, **Toko Bintang**, and **Android Store** need stronger identifying evidence before matching to online listings.
6. The summaries use nearby road names as context, but do not provide exact house numbers or coordinates. Do not assign coordinates by geocoding a generic road name.
