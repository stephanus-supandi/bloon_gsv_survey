## 1. Scope

This note records a return to Bellevue, Washington, after earlier Street View observations in Jakarta / Bekasi. The sequence revisits a July 2018 frame identified by the user as the original ClassifiedAds opening location, then compares Bellevue imagery from July 2024.

The central task is to track visible changes at locations the user identifies as corresponding across dates, without treating a visual match as proof of parcel boundaries, tenancy, ownership, or planning outcome.

## 2. Evidence and limitations

- All visual details in this note are reported from the user's descriptions of Google Street View frames.
- The underlying screenshots and panorama identifiers are not embedded in this file.
- Street View imagery date is distinct from the device status-bar time and battery percentage visible in a screenshot.
- Map pins and business labels are platform metadata, not physical evidence of a business's exact entrance, floor, ownership, or legal address.
- A sign reading PROPOSED LAND USE ACTION establishes that such a sign was visible in the described frame; it does not establish the proposal's content, approval, rejection, or outcome.
- Vegetation growth between two images is a visible change, but does not by itself establish the exact planting date, species, maintenance history, or land-use status.
- No original Street View imagery is redistributed in this record.

## 6. Analytical correction

An earlier interpretation suggested that Bellevue did not leave empty commercial space unused and instead rebranded it with visible business names. The more specific Wallace Properties comparison requires a narrower statement.

The 2024 frame includes a PROPOSED LAND USE ACTION notice, while the 2018 frame shows a marked visitor parking area and young trees. This supports recording a visible planning-related notice alongside the physical changes. It does not prove that the property was unused, that redevelopment was pending for the entire six years, or that the site was simply waiting.

Preferred wording:

> The Wallace Properties site is reported in two Street View periods, 2018 and 2024. The later view shows larger tree growth and a sign reading PROPOSED LAND USE ACTION. The proposal's subject and outcome, and the site's activity during the interval, remain unknown.

## 7. Comparison with Jakarta / Bekasi: keep the units comparable

The earlier Jakarta / Bekasi observations include narrow alleys, mixed residential-commercial frontages, motorcycles, local signage, and community-level notices. Bellevue observations include parking areas, commercial rows, freeway signage, and a car-dominated frame.

These scenes can be compared as documented street environments, but they are not equivalent samples. Avoid turning one Bellevue traffic frame or one Jakarta alley into a claim about either city's entire transport system, economy, or social character.

## 8. Suggested verification workflow

1. Preserve each original screenshot and record the Street View imagery month/year separately from device status-bar time.
2. Record panorama URLs or IDs, where available and permitted.
3. For the Wallace Properties comparison, verify the same viewpoint using fixed building corners, parking-bay geometry, curb lines, tree positions, and other stable features.
4. Transcribe the complete PROPOSED LAND USE ACTION notice only from a legible image; then seek the relevant City of Bellevue planning record before describing the proposal.
5. Verify business addresses and suite numbers from official business or property sources before assigning a physical unit.
6. Record map-pin position separately from the visible building, entrance, and official address.
7. Keep each claim labelled as REPORTED, OBSERVED, CORROBORATED, DISPUTED, UNKNOWN, or NOT_VERIFIED.

## 9. Working principles

> A map pin is not the place itself.

> A planning notice is not a planning outcome.

> A visible parking lot is not proof of inactivity.

> A single traffic frame is not a transport census.
