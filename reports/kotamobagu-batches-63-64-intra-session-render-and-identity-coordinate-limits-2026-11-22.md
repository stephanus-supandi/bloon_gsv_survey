Orange/gray ghost glyphs are treated as pipeline/UI overlays, not physical evidence.

### Provisional extensions and limits

1. **E7 — one POI, two frames, different pin-render visibility (Kost Imuk).** Img2 has no red pin visible in the described frame; Img3 has a visible pin on a pole. This is **absence-of-render**, not absence-of-POI, not evidence that the pin moved, and not a drift or projection test. UI rendering, view angle, zoom, occlusion, or clustering can make a pin visible in one frame and not another. The frames are treated as intra-session, not independent observations. A valid longitudinal/projection test would require evidence of the same coordinates across distinct passes or a record disappearing across dates; neither is supplied.
2. **E8 — number embedded in a listing name (“35.000”).** The number is part of the displayed name string. It cannot be paired with a rating, route duration, or another number to infer a shared entity, coordinate, or relationship. Shared numerals across different fields are not evidence of identity.
3. **Strongest in-frame match remains identity evidence only (Masjid Agung Baitul Makmur).** Exact, large facade signage increases confidence in the name/identity correspondence. It does not increase the evidence ceiling for coordinate accuracy. The same rule applies to weaker and stronger matches: match supports identity, not coordinate verification.
4. **Identical physical object as a session clue.** The blue becak in Img3 and Img4 is reported as the same object at the same moment, based on matching position, shape, and passenger. It supports grouping those frames as one capture moment; it is not independent corroboration of either POI or its pin.
5. **City suffixes are name content, not entity links.** “KOTAMOBAGU” in the COLUMBUS and mosque listing names is recorded as nomenclature. A city token in a name is not proof of pairing with a road label or another POI.
6. **Zone distribution is not a zone mechanism.** The fifth consecutive Kotamobagu batch does not establish that the zone has a distinct anchor or error mechanism. Only the described within-zone distribution may be recorded.
7. **NPC/background and pipeline layers remain separate.** The becak, truck, motorcycles, carts, umbrellas, trees, hills, neighboring MR.DIY/ORION signs, and property-sale banner are contextual objects or text, not automatic anchors for the selected POI. Gray ghost glyphs are UI/pipeline overlays, not anchors.
8. **Temporal cross-check remains absent.** July 2025 is the reported imagery badge. The recurring ©2025/©2026 Google watermark is a rendering artifact, not an independent date cross-check. No dated real-world object in the supplied descriptions confirms the imagery date.

**Batch 64 ceiling:** no **VERIFIED** case.
