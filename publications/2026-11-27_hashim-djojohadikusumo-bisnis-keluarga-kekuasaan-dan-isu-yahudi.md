## Article scope and principal content

The article presents Hashim Djojohadikusumo as a businessman and political figure whose public record merits examination across several distinct domains:

1. **Family and background** — son of Sumitro Djojohadikusumo and Dora Marie Sigar; younger brother of President Prabowo Subianto; grandson of Margono Djojohadikusumo, associated with the founding of Bank Negara Indonesia.
2. **Business history** — Tirtamas Group, Nations Energy, Nations Petroleum, and Arsari Group, founded in 2006. The company's official profile describes activity across sectors including cement, petrochemicals, plantations, mining, banking, commodity trading, agroforestry, digital infrastructure, renewable energy, and aquaculture.
3. **Tin and mining** — Arsari-affiliated mining and processing businesses, including companies reported in the tin value chain and the solder manufacturing business. The article distinguishes reported business links from independently verified shareholding and permit records.
4. **Tambang Mas Sangihe** — JATAM criticism of a proposed business relationship and Arsari's subsequent public denial of Hashim's shareholding/commissioner role and statement that the LOI was withdrawn. The proposed LOI is not treated as proof of ownership of PT Tambang Mas Sangihe.
5. **Digital infrastructure** — Hashim's former role as President Commissioner of PT Solusi Sinergi Digital Tbk (WIFI/Surge), and investment relationships reported through PT Arsari Sentra Data and PT Investasi Sukses Bersama.
6. **Political activity** — Hashim's role in Gerindra and reported support for Prabowo's presidential campaigns. Statements about campaign financing are distinguished from formal campaign-finance disclosures and legal findings.
7. **Government roles** — reported appointment as Special Presidential Envoy for Climate and Energy and other task-force/board roles. Potential conflict-of-interest questions are framed as governance questions, not findings of wrongdoing.
8. **Religion and the Jewish claim** — the sources reviewed do not substantiate the claim that Hashim is Jewish. A 2019 report quotes Hashim describing himself as Protestant Christian. Religion, ethnicity, business contact, and organizational affiliation are treated as separate categories.
9. **Swiss tax dispute** — reported Swiss tax proceedings and asset sales are described as a foreign tax dispute, not as proof of Indonesian corruption or a criminal conviction.
10. **Bank Industri matter (2002)** — historical reporting on detention/charges and the subsequent court action is treated cautiously; the record does not describe the matter as a conviction.
11. **ICW report** — the 2025 civil-society report on business interests surrounding Prabowo-Gibran is cited as an accountability analysis, not as a court judgment.
