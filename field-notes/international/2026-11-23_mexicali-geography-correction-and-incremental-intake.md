## 7. Evidence discipline

1. Geographic reclassification is recorded as a correction to the working dataset, with the observer's signage rationale preserved.
2. Listing ratings, review counts, hours, and categories are user-reported platform metadata, not independently verified.
3. A pin label or road name is not proof of a business identity or exact frontage.
4. Adjacent signs do not establish shared occupancy, ownership, or premises.
5. Different camera angles are re-observations unless a distinct entity is established.
6. Repeated screenshot batches do not increase the unique-POI count.
7. “Informal economy” is not assigned as a formal status based solely on storefront appearance or business scale.
8. No coordinates, business operating status, ownership, or present-day continuity are inferred.
9. No Street View imagery is redistributed in this repository; this note preserves textual descriptions only.
