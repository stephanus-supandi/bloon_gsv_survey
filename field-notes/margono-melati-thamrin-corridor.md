# Margono–Melati–Thamrin Corridor: Street View Field Notes

**Record type:** Qualitative Street View observation log  
**Project:** BLOON GSV Archaeology  
**Geographic scope:** Margono / Dukuh Pinggir – Waduk Melati – Thamrin Boulevard, Central Jakarta  
**Source basis:** Observer-described Street View frames and field notes  
**Status:** Working record; not an independently re-verified geospatial survey

## 1. Purpose and evidence classes

This record preserves observations across alleys, river-edge spaces, a commercial boulevard, and access-control points. It is not a cadastral survey, ownership investigation, business verification, sewer-network map, or proof of operational relationships between nearby sites.

- **OBSERVED** — directly described as visible in a Street View frame.
- **USER-IDENTIFIED** — identity/context explicitly supplied by the observer, not independently verified here.
- **INTERPRETATION** — a reading suggested by the frames.
- **UNVERIFIED / UNKNOWN** — not established by the available observations.

Do not upgrade an interpretation into fact merely because two features appear near one another or recur in different frames.

## 7. Cross-frame discipline

1. A map pin is metadata, not ground truth.
2. Spatial proximity does not establish ownership, operation, or infrastructure connectivity.
3. Similar-looking people in different frames are not assumed to be the same person.
4. Repeated labels do not establish one business entity or continuous location.
5. Visual resemblance does not establish an object's function.
6. Unreadable text remains unreadable; do not complete it by guesswork.
7. Street View capture labels/dates must be retained as supplied, not silently converted into exact survey dates.
8. No Street View imagery is redistributed here; only textual observations and analysis are preserved.
