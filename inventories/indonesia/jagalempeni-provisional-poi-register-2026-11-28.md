## Data limitations

- All names, categories, ratings, review counts, opening-hour states, imagery epochs, road associations, and screenshot/session display times are user-reported summaries. Original frames and listing cards were not independently inspected here.
- Ratings/review counts and hours are historical listing snapshots as reported, not current operating status.
- “Closed, opens at …” and “open 24 hours” are retained as reported listing metadata, not independently verified operating facts.
- The 6 hr 19 min motorbike values are UI travel-time metadata only. They do not establish coordinates, distance, route, or geographic separation.
- The wheelchair-accessibility icon is a reported UI indicator, not an independent accessibility audit.
- The firewood pile/shack description for Berkah Insan Sejahtera does not establish business activity, ownership, or the identity of the structure.
- The village sign is retained as reported context; no regency/province, exact address, coordinate, POI ID, pin-to-frontage match, or route order is assigned.
- No claims about ownership, tenancy, shared premises, chain affiliation, or corporate relationships are made.
- This is an area-level working register, **not yet the global consolidated master inventory**. No legacy master CSV, normalized POI records, coordinates, or map data were changed.
