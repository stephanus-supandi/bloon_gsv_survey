# BLOON GSV ARCHAEOLOGY — THE IMPOSSIBLE ARCHIVE

**A Future Films concept project**  
**Format:** Investigative mystery / archival thriller / analog-horror atmosphere  
**Status:** Concept development — not a factual claim about any person or place.

> WE FOUND THE RECORDS. WE CHECKED THE TIMELINE. WE STILL DON'T KNOW WHAT IT MEANS.

## The idea

A real-world Street View archaeology project becomes the foundation for a slow-burn mystery film. Ordinary streets, old images, timestamps, building changes, metadata, and gaps in the record are examined like evidence. The tension comes from the difference between what a record shows and what a viewer thinks it means.

The project must never invent evidence and present it as real. Verified material, uncertain observations, hypotheses, and fictional film devices must remain clearly separated.

## Why this is unsettling

- Each record looks ordinary by itself.
- Timelines may be incomplete or difficult to reconcile.
- A missing image is not proof that something was removed.
- Similar-looking places are not automatically the same place.
- A pattern is not proof of a connection.
- Some questions remain unanswered because the evidence cannot settle them.

## Evidence labels

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| VERIFIED | Directly supported by a traceable source or repeatable check |
| OBSERVED | Visible in a record, but not yet independently confirmed |
| HYPOTHESIS | A possible interpretation, not a finding |
| UNKNOWN | Available evidence does not settle the question |
| FICTION | Created for the film; never present it as real-world evidence |
